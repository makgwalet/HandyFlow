# Payroll Bureau: product decisions and phase 1 spec, 2026-11-26

## Decisions (all six accepted as the plan recommended)
1. Launch customer: independent bureaus and accounting practices.
2. Coverage: SA monthly, weekly and fortnightly. Multi-country later.
3. Client approval: configurable per client.
4. Employee self-service: client portal first, employee portal later. Employees keep getting payslips by email.
5. SARS filing: validated exports and guided filing first. No direct submission.
6. Calculation engine: one shared statutory capability, separate pay-run workflow per module.

## What the code already gives us
HR already has the shared pieces the bureau needs: approved, versioned tax-year rules (`hr_statutory_tax_years`, brackets, rebates), the record `shared.StatutoryYearRules`, a pure calculator `shared.SarsPayeCalculator` (bonus, back pay, employer benefits, medical credit by number of members), and an averaging method. The bureau still runs the older `SarsPayrollCalculator` with rates and year figures hard-coded in the class. So decision 6 means: move the bureau onto what HR already uses, not build a second engine.

## Phase 1 slices, in order
| Slice | What | Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Bureau calculates with the approved statutory rules through a shared `StatutoryRulesProvider` (HR implements it). Refuses a run when the year has no approved rules. Old hard-coded constants stop being used. | D7, D9 |
| 1b | Frozen calculation snapshot on every payslip: the rule year and its effective dates, the inputs used, and the formula version. Reprinting a payslip never recalculates from the live employee. | D6 |
| 1c | Pay groups and pay periods: a client has one or more pay groups (monthly, fortnightly, weekly), each with a period calendar and cut-off. A pay run belongs to a period of a group. | decision 2 |
| 1d | Periodic PAYE for weekly and fortnightly pay, and the year-to-date averaging method already in HR for monthly pay, so bonuses, part periods and mid-year starters tax correctly. | D8 |
| 1e | Pay components: a catalogue (earnings, deductions, benefits) with taxable and UIF/SDL flags and SARS source codes, per-employee values, in place of the fixed salary, travel, pension and medical fields. | engine 3 |

Each slice ships on its own, with tests, and does not change what an unchanged employee is paid, except where the old code was wrong. Slice 1a is the first one.

## Not in phase 1
Lifecycle states, maker-checker on runs, locking and reversal (phase 2, with D5). Bank files and payroll journals (phase 3). EMP201/EMP501/IRP5 exports (phase 4). Client approval step and client-portal payslip views (phase 5). Per-client pricing and profitability (phase 6).

## Risks to check as we go
- The old and new calculators may differ by cents on the same input (rounding, medical credit, UIF base). Slice 1a adds a comparison test for typical salaries and the differences are listed in the slice notes.
- The bureau must not depend on the `hr` module. The interface lives in `shared`; HR provides the implementation.

## Slice 1a delivered (patch 0184)
- `shared.StatutoryRulesProvider` is the one place payroll asks for a year's approved rules. HR's `StatutoryRulesService` implements it (no behaviour change there).
- `PayrollBureauEngine` now uses `SarsPayeCalculator` with those rules. No rates or year figures remain in it. A pay run for a year without approved rules is refused with 422 `NO_TAX_RULES`; the earlier check from patch 0180 (`NO_TAX_TABLES`) is replaced by this one.
- The old `SarsPayrollCalculator` is deprecated and unused.
- Behaviour that can differ from before, for the same employee: medical credit and UIF/SDL/retirement figures now come from the approved year row (2027 tax year: credit R376 not R364 was already in the old table; SDL threshold and UIF ceiling unchanged), rounding is the shared calculator's. Hand-checked cases: R20 000 a month gives PAYE R2 115.00; R30 000 a month with medical aid gives R4 305.00 (both in `PayrollBureauEngineTest`).
- Not run here: Maven, so the Spring wiring (one `StatutoryRulesProvider` bean) and the tests are unverified.

## Slice 1b delivered (patch 0185)
- Each new payslip records what it was calculated from (V950): the rule year's effective dates, a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact brackets and figures, the formula version (`shared-paye-1`), and the inputs the amounts do not show (date of birth, medical members, annual payroll used for the SDL threshold). It is recorded once, when the payslip is made; a second attempt is refused.
- `GET /pay-runs/{runId}/payslips/{payslipId}/calculation` returns this. For payslips made before V950 it says `recorded: false`; nothing is guessed for them.
- Payslip amounts were already stored, and the PDF already reads them, so a reprint did not recalculate. What was missing was the record of how they were reached; that is what this adds. Name, ID and bank details on a reprint still come from the live employee record.
- Not done: no screen shows the calculation yet, and the fingerprint is not printed on the PDF.

## Slice 1c delivered (patch 0186)
- Pay groups (V951): a client can have several groups, each MONTHLY, FORTNIGHTLY or WEEKLY, with an anchor date (first day of any period, for weekly and fortnightly), and the days between period end and pay day. Group names are unique per client among active groups, ignoring case.
- Employees and pay runs may belong to a group. No group means the client's default payroll, as before. Existing data is all ungrouped and behaves as it did, with one deliberate difference: an ungrouped run now pays only ungrouped employees, so moving someone into a group cannot pay them twice.
- A group's run must cover exactly one of the group's periods, and takes its tax year from the pay date (SARS taxes pay when it is paid). An ungrouped run keeps the old rule (tax year of the period start). Overlap checks are per group.
- Weekly and fortnightly runs can be created but are refused at processing (422 `FREQUENCY_NOT_SUPPORTED`) until slice 1d, because the calculator works on monthly salaries and would tax them wrongly.
- Endpoints: `GET/POST /clients/{id}/pay-groups`, `POST /pay-groups/{id}/archive` (refused while it has employees or a draft run), `GET /pay-groups/{id}/next-period`, `PUT /clients/{id}/employees/{employeeId}/pay-group`. `CreatePayRunRequest` has an optional `payGroupId`; responses carry `payGroupId`.
- Not done: no screens yet. They wait for 1d, since weekly groups cannot be processed before then. A client's old `payFrequency` field is not read by groups.

## Slice 1d delivered: periodic PAYE for weekly and fortnightly pay (2026-11-26)

- `SarsPayeCalculator.calculate(inputs, rules, periodsPerYear, uifBaseAlreadyThisMonth)`: annual equivalent = pay x 52 (weekly), 26 (fortnightly) or 12; PAYE is a period's share of the annual tax. The two-argument call (HR and monthly) is unchanged. Checked against SARS's 2027 weekly table: R4,991 a week gives R577.89 (table R578), R6,020 gives R845.43 (table R845).
- UIF: the ceiling is monthly (R17,712). Each payslip records `uif_base`; a later run in the same calendar month (by pay date) only charges UIF on what is left under the ceiling.
- The `FREQUENCY_NOT_SUPPORTED` refusal is gone. Payslips record `input_periods_per_year` and formula version `shared-paye-periodic-1` (monthly stays `shared-paye-1`). Migration V952.
- SDL threshold: the client's annual payroll is now the sum of each employee's pay x the periods a year of their own pay group, across all groups.
- **Meaning of employee amounts:** for an employee in a weekly or fortnightly group, gross, travel, pension and medical amounts on the employee record mean per pay period of that group.
- **Not done:** the year-to-date averaging method for monthly pay (HR has it; porting it is its own slice). Pay-group screens. Tests that use Mockito or Spring were not run (no Maven).

## Slice 1e delivered: pay components (2026-11-26)

Decisions taken with the product owner: components feed the current calculator (not a generic engine); one-off amounts can be entered per pay run; a standard South African set is made for each tenant on first use.

- **Catalogue** (V953, `pay_components`): code, name, role, optional SARS source code. The role fixes the treatment, so there are no separate tax/UIF/SDL switches that could contradict it:
  - `TAXABLE_EARNING` (overtime, commission, taxable allowances): added to the period's regular pay, annualised, liable for UIF and SDL.
  - `BONUS`: taxed by the bonus method (the extra tax the year bears once it is added); liable for UIF and SDL in the month paid.
  - `NON_TAXABLE_EARNING` (reimbursements): added to net pay only.
  - `DEDUCTION` (loan repayment, garnishee, union fees): taken from net pay after tax.
- **Standard set** (made on first `GET /pay-components`): Overtime 3607, Commission 3606, Other taxable earnings 3601, Bonus 3605, Expense reimbursement 3602, and four deductions with no code. Codes were checked against SARS's "Guide for Codes Applicable to Employees Tax Certificates" listing and a payroll vendor's table, not against the tax certificate specification for filing; check them before any IRP5 export (phase 4). A bureau can add its own components and archive any.
- **Per employee** (`GET/PUT /clients/{c}/employees/{e}/components`): an amount per pay period of the employee's group. **Per run** (`GET/PUT/DELETE /pay-runs/{id}/items`): DRAFT runs only.
- **Processing** copies each employee's lines to `pay_payslip_lines` (code, name, role, SARS code, source, amount), so editing the catalogue later cannot change a past payslip. `GET /pay-runs/{id}/payslips/{payslipId}/lines` returns them, and the payslip PDF prints them. Payslips gain `extra_earnings` and `extra_deductions`; total earnings, total deductions and net pay include them. Earlier payslips have 0 in both.
- The SDL threshold's annual payroll now includes recurring taxable components (not one-off items).
- **Deliberately not done:** the four fixed employee fields (salary, travel, pension, medical) stay as they are rather than moving into components. They feed the calculator's special rules (retirement cap, medical credit, travel taxable share) and salary changes are under approval control (D4), so moving them is a separate change. Payslip lines list only the component lines, not those four.
- **Open decision:** recurring components change what an employee is paid, but they are saved at once, not through the approval step that salary and bank changes use.
- Not done: screens; employer-paid benefits (fringe benefits) and leave pay as components; component values on the payroll journal (phase 3); no Maven run, so the Spring wiring and the Mockito tests are unverified.

## Phase 2 (first part) delivered: pay run lifecycle and reversal (2026-11-26; audit D5)

Decisions taken with the product owner: states Draft, Calculated, Approved, Finalised; approval by a different user with `PAYROLLBUREAU_APPROVE`; a finalised run is corrected by reversing it with a reason.

- **States** (V954): `DRAFT -> CALCULATED -> APPROVED -> FINALISED`, and `FINALISED -> REVERSED`. `CALCULATED` or `APPROVED` can be reopened to `DRAFT` (payslips and their lines are deleted; nothing has been sent). A finalised run cannot be reopened or edited.
- **Endpoints:** `POST /pay-runs/{id}/process` (calculates; now ends in CALCULATED and records who calculated it), `/reopen`, `/approve` (approve permission; refused if the same user calculated it, 403 `SELF_APPROVAL`), `/finalise`, `/reverse` (approve permission; reason required, 500 characters at most). Who and when for each step is kept on the run.
- **What waits for FINALISED:** emailing payslips (400 `PAYRUN_NOT_FINALISED`), the daily auto-send, and generating a fee note. Payslips can be viewed and downloaded for review from CALCULATED.
- **Reversal:** the run and its payslips stay on record. A reversed run no longer counts towards a month's UIF base for weekly or fortnightly runs. Nothing is posted anywhere yet, so nothing needs undoing elsewhere; when journals arrive (phase 3) reversal must post the opposite entries.
- **Existing data:** runs that were PROCESSED become FINALISED, with the finalised time set to when they were processed. Nobody approved them, so approved-by is empty for those.
- **Screen:** the pay runs list shows Calculate, Reopen, Approve, Finalise and Reverse by status, with status badges for the new states. Reverse asks for the reason in a browser prompt (to be replaced by a proper dialog).
- **Not done:** run exceptions and variance against the previous run (next part of phase 2); per-bureau switch to turn approval off (not asked for); reversal of payslips already emailed does not notify employees; `PAY_RUN_PROCESSED` notification type name unchanged; no Maven run, so Spring wiring is unverified.

## Phase 2 (second part) delivered: checks before a run and comparison with the previous run (2026-11-26)

- `GET /pay-runs/{id}/review`. For a DRAFT run it returns the checks; for a calculated run it returns the comparison with the client's previous run of the same pay group (the latest earlier run that has payslips). Computed on request, nothing stored.
- **Stops the run (422 `RUN_CHECKS_FAILED` when calculating):** no active employees; an employee with no pay set; another run of the same group, not reversed, that shares any day with this period (paying twice).
- **Warns only:** employee without a bank account, tax number or email; two employees with the same ID number; two employees paid into the same bank account.
- **Comparison warns on:** an employee not in the previous run, an employee missing from this one, and net pay that moved by 10% or more. The 10% is a starting point I chose, not a rule; it is `RunReviewRules.VARIANCE_THRESHOLD`.
- The pay runs list has a Check button on drafts and Compare on calculated runs, opening the findings in a dialog.
- **Tests I had left stale in slice 1d** (`PayGroupRunTest`, `PayrollBureauSafetyTest`) are rewritten: the old test expected weekly runs to be refused, and the old no-employee run is now blocked by the checks. They are Mockito tests and were not run.
- Not done: warnings do not need to be acknowledged before approving; the checks do not look at leave, terminations in the period or part-period pay; the checks are not repeated at approval.

## Phase 3 (first part) delivered: bank payment file and payroll journal (2026-11-26)

Decisions taken with the product owner: a generic CSV bank file only (no bank-specific layouts yet); the journal goes to the client as a downloadable file, not into HandyFlow accounting; bank files need the approve permission, one active file per run, a reason for a replacement, every download recorded.

- **Bank file** (V955): `POST /pay-runs/{id}/bank-files` for a FINALISED run, one CSV line per employee: employee number, beneficiary name, bank, branch code, account number, net pay, reference (`run number + employee number`). Employees with no bank account or no net pay are left out and listed in the response. A second file needs a reason and marks the first SUPERSEDED. `GET .../bank-files` lists them; `GET .../bank-files/{id}/download` returns the file and records who downloaded it and when. The file is stored encrypted (it carries account numbers). A race that would make two active files is stopped by a unique index (409 `BANK_FILE_CONFLICT`).
- **Journal:** `GET /pay-runs/{id}/journal` (CSV). Debits: salaries and wages (all earnings), employer UIF, SDL. Credits: PAYE, UIF (employee and employer), SDL, medical aid, retirement fund, other deductions, net salaries payable. It is checked to balance before it is handed over. For a reversed run the same journal comes back with debits and credits swapped. Account names are suggestions; there is no per-client mapping to a chart of accounts yet.
- Cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` are prefixed with an apostrophe so a spreadsheet does not run them.
- **Not done:** bank-specific formats (each needs the bank's current file specification); payment status or reconciliation after the bank file is sent; per-client account mapping; posting into HandyFlow accounting; employer-paid medical or retirement contributions in the journal (the bureau does not record them); no Maven run, so Spring wiring and the encrypted-column round trip are unverified, and the V955 migration was run on the scratch database only.

## Screens for pay groups and pay items delivered (2026-11-26)

- **Pay Groups tab:** list, create (weekly, fortnightly or monthly, first day of a period, days to pay day), archive. A note says amounts on the employee record are per pay period for weekly and fortnightly groups.
- **Employees tab:** a Pay group column to move an employee between groups, and a Pay items button for their recurring components.
- **Pay Items tab:** the component catalogue (shared by all clients of the bureau): create, archive, treatment and SARS code shown.
- **New pay run:** a group picker that fills the period and pay date from the group's next period and sends the group with the run. Before this the dialog could not create a run for a group at all. The runs list shows the group's name.
- **Draft runs:** an Items button for one-off amounts (overtime, a bonus, a reimbursement, a deduction).
- The shared dialog, table and style pieces moved out of `PayrollBureauPage.tsx` into `ui.tsx` and `uiStyles.ts`. No behaviour changed; the page has fewer lint errors than before (65, was 67).
- Not done: the calculation record (rules, formula, UIF base) is still not shown anywhere; payslip lines are not shown in the run's payslip table; the screens were not opened in a browser, only unit-tested with mocked data (6 new tests).

## Phase 4 (first part) delivered: ETI calculation and EMP201 monthly figures (2026-11-26)

Decisions taken with the product owner: scope is the whole of SARS filing including EMP501 and tax certificates (this part covers ETI and EMP201 only); the system produces figures and validated exports for guided filing on eFiling, with no direct submission; a tax month is the client's FINALISED runs whose pay date falls in the month (reversed runs are left out; weekly and fortnightly runs count in the month they are paid); ETI is built now; the client's right to claim is a flag set by staff; minimum wage is a per-employee flag (default: meets it); ETI facts are fields on the employee record.

- **ETI rules** (`EtiRules`, pure, 11 tests pass): bands are data in a `RuleSet` with an effective date, seeded with the set in force from 1 April 2025 (below R2,500: 60% of pay in the first 12 qualifying months, 30% in the second 12; R2,500 to R5,499.99: R1,500 / R750; R5,500 to R7,499.99: R1,500 - 75% of the amount over R5,500 / R750 - 37.5% of it; nothing from R7,500). Tests: age at the end of the month (18 to 29), SA ID, asylum or refugee ID, employed on or after 1 October 2013, not a domestic worker, not a connected person, minimum wage met, at most 24 months, part-month gross-up to 160 hours and apportionment, scheme end on 28 February 2029. A new SARS table is a new `RuleSet`, not an edit to the bands. Source: SARS ETI page and ETI guide ETI01 issue 5, fetched 2026-11-26.
- **ID facts** (`EtiFactsRules`, 4 tests pass): checks a 13-digit ID (date and check digit), reads the date of birth from it when the employee record has none, and picks the ID kind (the manual choice wins, else SA ID if valid, else OTHER).
- **Data (V956):** employee `eti_id_kind`, `domestic_worker`, `connected_person`, `below_minimum_wage`, `eti_months_prior` (months already claimed before joining the bureau); client `eti_claimable` (default off), note, who and when; `pay_emp201_returns` (one per client and month, DRAFT or FILED) and `pay_emp201_eti_lines` (one per employee: eligible or not, tier, pay, incentive, reason codes, rule version).
- **EMP201:** `POST /clients/{c}/emp201/{yyyy-MM}/prepare` adds up PAYE, UIF (employee and employer) and SDL from the month's finalised payslips, works out ETI per employee on their pay across the month, and gives PAYE less ETI used, plus UIF and SDL, as the amount payable, due on the 7th of the next month or the business day before (existing holiday table). It can be run again until the return is filed. `POST .../file` (approve permission) records the eFiling reference and fixes the figures. `GET /clients/{c}/emp201?taxYear=2027` lists a tax year (March to February); `GET .../{yyyy-MM}` gives one with its ETI lines.
- **ETI claim:** ETI is always calculated and shown, but is taken off the PAYE only when staff have marked the client as allowed to claim (`PUT /clients/{c}/eti-claim`, approve permission; switching it off needs a note). Months count towards the 24 only when actually claimed. Unused ETI (more than the month's PAYE) is carried to the next month of the same tax year and is not carried across February.
- **Employee ETI facts:** `PUT /clients/{c}/employees/{e}/eti`.
- **Assumptions to check:** pay used for ETI is the payslip's total earnings (salary, travel allowance and recurring or one-off earnings), not a SARS-defined "remuneration" figure; no hours are recorded, so part-month gross-up never applies (an employee who starts or leaves mid-month is treated as a full month, which can overstate ETI); the SARS roll-over is limited to four months, which is not modelled (only the end of the tax year); refunds of unused ETI on the EMP501 are not modelled; special economic zone rules are not modelled; the minimum wage is a staff flag, not tested against hours; the due-date rule is the one already used by the deadline engine.
- **Not done:** screens (claim flag, employee ETI facts, EMP201 list); EMP501 reconciliation; IRP5/IT3(a) tax-certificate data; Mockito test for `Emp201Service` (not written); no Maven run, so Spring wiring and the JPQL are unverified; V956 ran on the scratch database only and entities validate against it.

## Phase 4 (second part) delivered: EMP501 reconciliation and tax-certificate figures (2026-11-26)

- **What it is:** `GET /clients/{c}/emp501?period=INTERIM|ANNUAL&taxYear=2027` works out, on request and without storing anything, one certificate (IRP5 or IT3(a)) per employee from the client's FINALISED runs paid in the period (interim March to August, annual March to February; the year is the one the tax year ends in), and reconciles the PAYE, UIF and SDL on them with the client's EMP201 returns. It also gives ETI used and the unused ETI at the period end (SARS refunds that on the reconciliation). `GET .../emp501/working-paper` (approve permission) is a CSV of the certificate figures for review.
- **Certificate figures** (`TaxCertificateRules`, 8 tests pass): income by SARS code (salary 3601, travel allowance 3701, then each pay item by its own SARS code; non-taxable items default to 3602, taxable ones without a code to 3601), non-taxable total 3696, gross taxable 3699, PAYE 4102, UIF 4141 (employee plus employer), SDL 4142, and 4149 as the sum of the three. IRP5 when PAYE was deducted, otherwise IT3(a).
- **Stops (BLOCK):** a code that is not valid for 2027; no income tax reference number (SARS has refused reconciliations without one since February 2026); PAYE above gross taxable income; a month with finalised runs but no prepared EMP201. **Warns:** unchecked codes, an EMP201 not marked filed, a difference between the EMP201 figures and the certificates (SARS asks for a reason), no ID number, no reason for non-deduction on an IT3(a).
- **Sources read on 2026-11-26:** SARS guides PAYE-AE-06-G06 (codes 2027), PAYE-AE-06-G07 (validation rules 2027), EMP-GEN-02-G01 (employer reconciliation process), PAYE-AE-06-G08, and SARS's interim filing season notices (21 September to 31 October 2026; annual 1 April to 31 May).
- **Found while checking the codes:** SARS's 2027 code list gives code 3605 (annual payment or bonus) as applying from 2003 to 2018 only, and the page I could read does not say which code replaces it. The standard Bonus pay item is seeded with 3605, so a bonus makes a certificate fail the check. **Needs a decision from someone who knows the current code list (or the bureau's accountant) before the Bonus item's code is changed;** I have not changed it.
- **Not done:**
  - SARS's tax-certificate CSV and the EMP501 declaration layout. The layout is in SARS's PAYE Employer Reconciliation BRS (V25.3.0 for the 2026 interim), which I did not read. SARS's guide describes it only in outline (code,data pairs ending in 9999, one employer per file, at most 20 income and 12 deduction codes per certificate), so I have not generated that file.
  - Pension and medical aid codes (4001, 4005) are from memory and flagged on every certificate that has them; the fund type (pension, provident, retirement annuity) is not recorded.
  - ETI codes 7002 to 7009 and the ETI indicator on certificates, directive numbers (3230), the EMP501 "payments made" section (the system does not record payments to SARS), SEZ codes, certificate numbering, interim certificates for leavers, the EMP601 cancellation and EMP701 adjustment.
  - Screens for EMP201, ETI and EMP501.
  - No Maven run and no test for the services; the JPQL is unverified. There is no migration in this part.
