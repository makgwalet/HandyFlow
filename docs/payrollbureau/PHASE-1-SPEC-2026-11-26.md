# Payroll Bureau: product decisions and phase 1 spec, 2026-11-26

## Decisions (all six accepted as the plan recommended)
1. Launch customer: independent bureaus and accounting practices.
2. Coverage: SA monthly, weekly and fortnightly. Multi-country later.
3. Client approval: configurable per client.
4. Employee self-service: client portal first, employee portal later. Employees keep getting payslips by email.
5. SARS filing: validated exports and guided filing first. No direct submission.
6. Calculation engine: one shared statutory capability, separate pay-run workflow per module.

## What the code already gives us
HR already has the shared pieces the bureau needs: approved, versioned tax-year rules (`hr_statutory_tax_years`, brackets, rebates), the record `shared.StatutoryYearRules`, a pure calculator `shared.SarsPayeCalculator` (bonus, back pay, employer benefits, medical credit by number of members), and an averaging method. The bureau still runs the older `SarsPayrollCalculator` with rates and year figures hard-coded in the class. So decision 6 means: move the bureau onto what HR already uses, not build a second engine.

## Phase 1 slices, in order
| Slice | What | Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Bureau calculates with the approved statutory rules through a shared `StatutoryRulesProvider` (HR implements it). Refuses a run when the year has no approved rules. Old hard-coded constants stop being used. | D7, D9 |
| 1b | Frozen calculation snapshot on every payslip: the rule year and its effective dates, the inputs used, and the formula version. Reprinting a payslip never recalculates from the live employee. | D6 |
| 1c | Pay groups and pay periods: a client has one or more pay groups (monthly, fortnightly, weekly), each with a period calendar and cut-off. A pay run belongs to a period of a group. | decision 2 |
| 1d | Periodic PAYE for weekly and fortnightly pay, and the year-to-date averaging method already in HR for monthly pay, so bonuses, part periods and mid-year starters tax correctly. | D8 |
| 1e | Pay components: a catalogue (earnings, deductions, benefits) with taxable and UIF/SDL flags and SARS source codes, per-employee values, in place of the fixed salary, travel, pension and medical fields. | engine 3 |

Each slice ships on its own, with tests, and does not change what an unchanged employee is paid, except where the old code was wrong. Slice 1a is the first one.

## Not in phase 1
Lifecycle states, maker-checker on runs, locking and reversal (phase 2, with D5). Bank files and payroll journals (phase 3). EMP201/EMP501/IRP5 exports (phase 4). Client approval step and client-portal payslip views (phase 5). Per-client pricing and profitability (phase 6).

## Risks to check as we go
- The old and new calculators may differ by cents on the same input (rounding, medical credit, UIF base). Slice 1a adds a comparison test for typical salaries and the differences are listed in the slice notes.
- The bureau must not depend on the `hr` module. The interface lives in `shared`; HR provides the implementation.

## Slice 1a delivered (patch 0184)
- `shared.StatutoryRulesProvider` is the one place payroll asks for a year's approved rules. HR's `StatutoryRulesService` implements it (no behaviour change there).
- `PayrollBureauEngine` now uses `SarsPayeCalculator` with those rules. No rates or year figures remain in it. A pay run for a year without approved rules is refused with 422 `NO_TAX_RULES`; the earlier check from patch 0180 (`NO_TAX_TABLES`) is replaced by this one.
- The old `SarsPayrollCalculator` is deprecated and unused.
- Behaviour that can differ from before, for the same employee: medical credit and UIF/SDL/retirement figures now come from the approved year row (2027 tax year: credit R376 not R364 was already in the old table; SDL threshold and UIF ceiling unchanged), rounding is the shared calculator's. Hand-checked cases: R20 000 a month gives PAYE R2 115.00; R30 000 a month with medical aid gives R4 305.00 (both in `PayrollBureauEngineTest`).
- Not run here: Maven, so the Spring wiring (one `StatutoryRulesProvider` bean) and the tests are unverified.

## Slice 1b delivered (patch 0185)
- Each new payslip records what it was calculated from (V950): the rule year's effective dates, a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact brackets and figures, the formula version (`shared-paye-1`), and the inputs the amounts do not show (date of birth, medical members, annual payroll used for the SDL threshold). It is recorded once, when the payslip is made; a second attempt is refused.
- `GET /pay-runs/{runId}/payslips/{payslipId}/calculation` returns this. For payslips made before V950 it says `recorded: false`; nothing is guessed for them.
- Payslip amounts were already stored, and the PDF already reads them, so a reprint did not recalculate. What was missing was the record of how they were reached; that is what this adds. Name, ID and bank details on a reprint still come from the live employee record.
- Not done: no screen shows the calculation yet, and the fingerprint is not printed on the PDF.
