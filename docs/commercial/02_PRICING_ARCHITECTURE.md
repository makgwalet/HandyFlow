# Pricing architecture (recommendation, not approved)

Rand figures are deliberately absent except where they exist in the seeds or the 29 Oct audit and are labelled so. Real prices need the cost and margin inputs listed in section 12. **R** = recommended value; **A** = needs business approval.

## 1. Structure
* **Workspace plan** (platform): Starter, Growth, Business, Enterprise. Governs seats, storage, API and automation allowances, support level, and the Free Core meters. **Starter is included free with the first paid module (R/A).** Growth and Business carry a platform fee priced from cost per seat, storage and support (A).
* **Free Core** (CRM, Invoicing, Catalogue core features): a derived entitlement, section 2.
* **Specialist modules**: one standard monthly price each at launch; banded metered prices only where the meter drives cost (HR per employee; Accountant and Compliance Services per active client; Internal Audit per seat; Compliance Tender per active tender). Starting bands are the 29 Oct proposals, **from evidence, not approved**.
* **Bundles** and **negotiated Enterprise contracts** sit on top; both reference existing module SKUs.

## 2. Conditional Free Core: rules
Free Core is a computed row, `source = DERIVED_FREE_CORE`, never a purchasable module.

| Situation | Free Core |
|---|---|
| No paid module, no trial | **Not entitled**; read-only transition then locked (section 7) |
| One paid module ACTIVE | Entitled |
| Three paid modules | Entitled **once** |
| Cancels one of several | Retained while at least one other qualifying module is ACTIVE or in approved TRIAL |
| Cancels the last paid module | Transition (section 7): 30 days read-only with export, then locked; records preserved |
| Approved trial | Entitled for the trial length |
| Trial expires unpaid | Same transition as cancelling the last module |
| Module in PAST_DUE grace | Still qualifies during grace; suspended when the module is suspended |
| Complimentary or Enterprise-contract module | Qualifies if the contract marks it `qualifies_free_core` (A) |

**Which modules qualify?** Recommend every **priced** specialist module qualifies, and the R0 rows do not until priced (D-06), otherwise a R0 module becomes a free route to Free Core. Recalculation runs on every subscription event (section 3 of `03`), never on a nightly job alone.

## 3. Tiers, seats and metering: recommended starting values
Earlier proposals conflicted (25/15, 100/50, Essential 50 invoices). These are starting values **for calibration after 90 days of real usage**, not commercial truth.

| Workspace tier | Active seats (R/A) | Quotes issued / month | Invoices issued / month | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Starter (included with first paid module) | 5 | 100 | 50 | Matches 29 Oct (50) and your earlier Starter row |
| Growth | 20 | 500 | 250 | |
| Business | 50 | 2,000 | 1,000 | |
| Enterprise | negotiated | negotiated | negotiated | contract |

* **Assessment.** For a South African sole proprietor 50 invoices a month is generous, but for a contractor or retailer billing weekly it is tight. For the businesses that will pay for HR, Accounting or Clinic, 50 issued invoices is likely too low and will create support tickets. **Recommendation:** when a tenant holds the paid **Finance** bundle (Invoicing paid tier), the Invoicing meter is lifted to the bundle's limit, because the invoice is then a paid feature, not the free hook. Otherwise the caps punish exactly the tenants who pay most.
* **Free Core inherits the workspace tier's limits**; there is no separate "Free" tier row any more (retires the 25/15 idea).
* **Modules have their own meters** only where a cost driver exists (employees, clients, tenders, storage, SMS, AI calls). Do not meter everything.
* **Hard or soft?** (R/A) Quotations: hard cap with upgrade message. Invoices: **soft cap**: warn at 80%, 90%, 100%; allow up to 110% for the period with an on-screen notice; then block *issuing* until upgrade or the new period; credit notes, payments, viewing, PDF download and drafts are never blocked. A hard cap on invoicing can stop a business collecting money or meeting VAT duties.

## 4. What counts (usage rules)
Adopt your listed rules, with these clarifications:

| Action | Quote meter | Invoice meter |
|---|---|---|
| Save or edit a draft | 0 | 0 |
| Issue or send a quote | +1 (first issue only) | 0 |
| Issue or finalise an invoice | 0 | +1 |
| Convert issued quote to invoice | already counted | +1 (when the invoice is issued, not when drafted) |
| Re-send, edit after issue, view, download | 0 | 0 |
| Credit note, correction | 0 | 0 |
| Void or delete an issued document | **does not restore** | **does not restore** |
| Recurring invoice generated by schedule | | +1 each (paid feature, but metered) |

Usage belongs to the tenant. A period is the tenant's billing month; day boundaries are in Africa/Johannesburg. Enforcement is transactional on the server (section 6 of `03`). A **purchasable top-up pack** is possible (A) but not recommended at launch; prefer upgrade.

## 5. Seat policy (R/A)
| Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Active seat | A user who can sign in (status ACTIVE) |
| Owner / administrators | Count |
| Invited, not accepted | **Do not count** until accepted, but the number of live invitations plus active users cannot exceed the limit (what the code does today: live invitations are reserved) |
| Expired or cancelled invitations | Do not count (fixed in patch 0606) |
| Suspended or deactivated | Do not count; reactivation re-checks the limit |
| External client / portal users, suppliers, auditors, accountants | **Do not count** (separate, unlimited, but rate-limited); a "contractor" who works inside the system **does count** |
| Platform support users | Never count; always audited |
| Downgrade below current users | Existing users stay; **new invites blocked**; admin chooses whom to deactivate. Never auto-delete |
| Employee leaves | Deactivate; seat frees at once; history retained |

## 6. Trials (R/A)
* **30 days standard**, configured at tenant creation by module list, not "all modules". Only modules the tenant selects (maximum configurable, default 3) enter trial. Free Core included during an approved trial.
* **Payment details not required** at start (A, trade-off: conversion vs abuse). One trial per tenant per module; a repeat needs a platform administrator, with a reason, recorded.
* **Extensions** by a platform administrator only, with reason, recorded in `entitlement_events`.
* **Production use** during trial: full functionality, but outbound cost-bearing features (SMS, AI) metered and capped.
* **Subscribe before expiry:** trial converts at once; the paid period starts that day (no double charge); trial days are not refunded or carried over.
* **Add or remove a module during trial:** allowed; module trial end is capped to the account trial (existing behaviour, kept).
* The hard-coded `60` appears in `ModuleController`, `BillingEventHandlers` and `OnboardingModuleSelection`; move it to one configuration value.

## 7. Lifecycle and downgrade
Separate **module subscription** status from **tenant** status. They are different events and must not share one enum.

**Module subscription:** `TRIAL → ACTIVE → PAST_DUE (grace 7 days, existing) → SUSPENDED → CANCELLED`; plus `CANCELLING` (cancelled, still active until period end) and `EXPIRED` (trial ended unpaid). Today the code has `PILOT, ACTIVE, PAST_DUE, SUSPENDED, CANCELLED` at account level and `TRIAL, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, CANCELLED` at module level.

**Tenant:** `ACTIVE`, `TRANSITION_READ_ONLY` (30 days), `SUSPENDED` (non-payment, access blocked, data kept), `CLOSED` (customer asked; export offered, retention applies).

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Payment fails | PAST_DUE for 7 days with banner and emails; full access; then SUSPENDED |
| Module cancelled | Access to the end of the paid period; then module locked; records preserved; dependants warned |
| Trial expires unpaid | EXPIRED; transition |
| Last paid module ends | Free Core ends after transition |
| **Transition (30 days)** | Existing records readable and exportable; new restricted operations blocked; credit notes, payments and statutory exports still allowed; renew or upgrade restores at once |
| After transition | Locked for use, **data retained**, access to export on request; no deletion because a subscription ended |
| Tenant closure | Export, then retention schedule |

Retention: South African tax, VAT, payroll and company-law retention periods, POPIA obligations and e-record requirements must be **confirmed by a tax or legal professional (A)**. Nothing here is a compliance claim. Do not hard-code tax rates in the entitlement model (VAT is read from `VatRateProvider` already).

## 8. Discounts, bundles, promotions
Existing engine: partnership > volume > code, best single discount, none stack, locked on the module row at activation. Recommendation:
1. **Keep** the non-stacking rule and lock-in.
2. **Choose one schedule** between the live ladder (3 / 5 / 8 / 12 modules → 5 / 10 / 15 / 20%) and your proposal (2 / 3-4 / 5+ → 10 / 15 / 20%). The proposal discounts earlier and more; model margin first (D-09).
3. **Bundles** reference module SKUs and are priced at a bundle rate; a tenant cannot buy a bundle and a member module separately (unique constraint on active module per tenant). When a bundle and the ladder both apply, the better of the two applies, not both. Candidate bundles (to validate): Finance (Accounting, AP, Invoicing paid tier, Expenses), People (HR, Recruiter, Desk), Industry (Security, Fleet, Fuel, Earthmoving), Agency (Creative, Contracting, Tasks, Marketing).
4. **Promotions** have start, end, eligibility, redemption limit and cannot grant access beyond what price pays for; they affect price, never permissions.
5. **Enterprise**: a contract record lists modules and limits; future modules are **not** automatically included (they must be added to the contract). All-modules access is a negotiated product, not a default.
6. **Grandfathering**: the price lives on the subscription row as a snapshot; the catalogue price can change without touching existing subscribers. Version the price book (today prices are edited in place).
7. **Annual**: schema allows `ANNUAL`, unused; offer with a clear discount after monthly billing is stable.
8. **Professional free-module credit**: retire.

## 9. Information needed to set real prices
Hosting and database cost per tenant; storage, SMS, e-mail and AI cost per tenant per month; support hours per tenant; payment fees; competitor prices per module for South Africa (Xero, Sage, Zoho, SimplePay data exists in the 29 Oct audit; none for audit, tender, bureau or clinic); target margin; expected modules per tenant; churn assumptions; pilot customers' willingness to pay.

## 10. Enterprise model
Named contract with modules, seats, limits, support level, term, price, and renewal. Negotiated items: SSO, retention terms, dedicated support, SLA, custom limits. Governance (maker-checker, scoped access) is part of the IAM roadmap, not a paid-only extra for basic security.
