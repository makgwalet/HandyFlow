## 1. Current commercial implementation (verified from code)

* **Plans.** Three seeded plans, Essential, Professional, Enterprise (R599, R1,299, R2,999 per the 22 Oct audit), each with `max_users` (5, 20, unlimited) and a `features` JSON.
* **Modules.** `module_catalogue` (key, name, monthly price, category), `plan_modules`, `tenant_modules` (status TRIAL, ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, CANCELLED, with `discount_pct` and `discount_source`), and a separate `module_subscriptions` table.
* **Entitlement.** `EntitlementService.isModuleActive` grants access if **any of four sources** says yes: a boolean in the plan JSON, `plan_modules`, `tenant_modules`, or `module_subscriptions`. There is no single source of truth.
* **Subscription states.** `PILOT, ACTIVE, PAST_DUE, SUSPENDED, CANCELLED`. A nightly job (02:00 Africa/Johannesburg) ends expired pilots; a second (02:30) suspends tenants whose 7-day past-due grace has run out. Module trials are capped to the account trial (60 days by default; 60 is hard-coded in three places).
* **Discounts.** A real engine exists: partnership %, volume tiers (3+ modules 5%, 5+ 10%, 8+ 15%, 12+ 20%), and codes. Best single discount wins; none stack. The discount is locked in on the module row at activation.
* **Enforcement.** `FeatureGuard.requireModule` in about 244 of 473 controller files; **229 controller files had no module check** (now partly covered at runtime by report-mode patch 0608). `requireFeature` has no callers.
* **Usage limits.** The plan JSON defines `max_invoices_per_month` and `max_customers`; **nothing reads them** (verified in the 22 Oct audit; no billing tests or code were added since). Only the user-seat limit is enforced, at invitation.
* **Billing mechanics.** Monthly tenant invoices are generated by a scheduled job and **marked paid by hand**. No payment gateway exists.
* **Tests.** There are no tests for `EntitlementService`, `SubscriptionService` or `BillingScheduler` (verified now: the only test in the `billing` test folder is the one added in patch 0608).
* **Frontend.** One 460-line `BillingPage` (subscription, My Modules, add and cancel with a cancel preview). No usage dashboard, no plan comparison, no upgrade or downgrade preview, no trial management, no payment history screen (verified now by reading the file and the route list).

### 2.1 Inconsistencies inside the June documents (to fix before reuse)

1. **Discount at two add-ons.** The worked example in the Pricing Strategy applies 10% at two modules; its "Question 1" answer says "if 2 modules: no volume discount yet"; its annual combination table says 10% at two modules. Pick one.
2. **Totals disagree.** The same example is shown as R2,871, R2,869.95 and R2,869. Rounding or a different add-on list; recompute from the catalogue.
3. **Dunning timeline.** PAST_DUE starts on day 8 with a 7-day grace, so grace ends on day 15, yet the timeline says day 14. Non-payment also ends in CANCELLED at day 30 with 90-day retention, whereas the code stops at SUSPENDED and never cancels automatically.
4. **Credit meaning.** The Platform Analysis asks whether `included_module_count` means "included in price" or "total allowed" and proposes Essential 1 and Professional 2; the Pricing Strategy says Essential 0 and Professional 1. The Pricing Strategy is the later, more specific document and is used here.
5. **Unsourced claim.** "Zendesk tried forced tier upgrades in 2019 and saw a 23% increase in churn" is not supported by any source I can check. Do not repeat it externally.
6. **Legal remarks.** The playbook suggests the CCMA for small debts. The CCMA deals with employment disputes, not customer debts. This must be corrected by a lawyer (small claims or magistrate's court is the usual route). Contract wording, refund rules and the early-termination fee also need legal and Consumer Protection Act review.
7. **Enterprise arithmetic.** The "upgrade to Enterprise" sales message relies on a R128 difference between R2,871 and R2,999. That comparison holds only for that one add-on list.

**Contradiction to resolve:** on 4 Nov you approved "CRM, Invoicing and Catalogue are free entry tiers". Today's brief says Free Core is **conditional on one paid module**. I have treated today's brief as current and updated the IAM documents accordingly (`ENTITLEMENT_TIERS_AND_GAPS.md` section 2 item 2 and the free-tier points). A tenant with no paid module and no trial then has **no ongoing access**, which affects sole proprietors who only want invoicing. That is a commercial choice, see D-01.

## 3. Main conflicts and defects

1. Four overlapping entitlement sources; one module key mismatch (`supply_chain` vs `supplychain`); at least five built modules whose keys are not in the catalogue and so cannot be sold (22 Oct F4; re-check with the query in `04`).
2. 229 controller files without a module check (P0 commercial and security).
3. Seven catalogue rows priced R0 in the migrations (Collections Agency, Debt Collection, Internal Audit, Legal Compliance, Warehousing, Insurance, Insurance Brokerage) with no stated decision.
4. Marketing exists in code and the catalogue but is **not in your 41-module list**. Either add it or retire it; see `01`.
5. No usage metering anywhere; nothing prevents unlimited quotes or invoices.
6. Trial length is hard-coded (60) in the controller, the event handler and the module service.
7. Downgrade and cancellation treat everything the same way: a module is simply set CANCELLED; a tenant is PAST_DUE then SUSPENDED. There is no read-only transition, no export prompt and no distinct "tenant closure".
8. Statutory content is not tiered: a good rule already in the 29 Oct audit and kept here (section 5 of `02`).

## 4. Recommended commercial model (needs your approval)

**Hybrid: one workspace plan plus paid specialist modules, with a derived Free Core.**

* **Workspace plan** (Starter, Growth, Business, Enterprise) controls seats, storage, API and automation allowances, support level and the Free Core meters. **Starter is included at no extra charge with the first paid module.** Growth and Business add a platform fee, because they carry real cost (seats, storage, support). This avoids a base fee that a sole trader pays before getting any value, while keeping a clear upgrade path.
* **Free Core** (CRM, Invoicing, Catalogue core features) is **derived**: present while the tenant holds at least one ACTIVE paid specialist module or an approved trial. It is never sold, never duplicated, and is recomputed whenever a subscription changes.
* **Specialist modules** launch at **one standard price each**, except where a meter already exists and drives cost: HR & Payroll per employee, Accountant and Compliance Services per active client, Internal Audit per seat, Compliance Tender per active tender. These are the bands from the 29 Oct audit and are marked "from evidence, not approved".
* **Bundles** (Finance, People, Industry, Agency) reference existing module SKUs; a bundle price replaces the volume ladder.

Why not the other two: **Option 1** (paid platform plan plus modules) taxes the sole proprietor and invites "why am I paying for nothing"; **Option 2** (module-led only) leaves seats, storage and support with no commercial home and makes the Free Core limits arbitrary.

## 5. Major risks

| Risk | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Enforcing entitlement before data is clean | Trial, internal and demo tenants use modules without subscription rows; strict enforcement locks them out. Hence report mode first (patch 0608) |
| Free Core conditionality | A tenant cancelling its last module loses invoices access. Mitigation: read-only transition plus export (section 7 of `02`) |
| Invoice caps and statutory duty | Blocking a business from issuing a legally required invoice or credit note at month-end is a customer and a legal risk. Recommend soft cap on invoices, no cap on credit notes |
| Billing integrity | Manual mark-paid, no gateway, no tests on the lifecycle |
| Pricing not evidence-based | Seeded numbers disagree with each other; no cost model |
| Permission model | The default `ADMIN` snapshot and ungated modules mean entitlement and authorisation are not yet independent controls (see IAM documents) |
