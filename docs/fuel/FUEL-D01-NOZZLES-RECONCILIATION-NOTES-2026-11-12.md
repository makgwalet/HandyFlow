# Fuel D01 - Tank-to-pump reconciliation and nozzle grouping (2026-11-12)
**Implemented (1) Tank-to-pump reconciliation:** for a tank and a period, opening dip + every recorded movement (grouped: supplier receipts, forecourt sales by pump meters, dispatches, deliveries, transfers, adjustments, reversals) = what should be in the tank; the closing dip is compared with it and the difference shown in litres and as a % of litres out. Separately, pump-meter litres for the same period are compared with the stock ledger's forecourt sales (they should agree). Read-only; posts nothing. Web: collapsed "Tank reconciliation" panel on the Forecourt tab.
**Implemented (2) Nozzle grouping:** each pump record can name a dispenser and a nozzle number (1-20, unique per dispenser, case-insensitive). Shown in the pump list and edit dialog.
**Important design choice:** the existing "pump" is already one metered dispensing point with its own meter, tank and price, i.e. a nozzle. I did NOT rebuild shifts to read per nozzle: that would rework the verified open/close/stock-posting flow for no behavioural gain. Nozzle grouping is a label layer. If you want multi-nozzle dispensers with a separate physical-pump entity (calibration records, dispenser-level downtime), that is a larger change to confirm first.
**No built-in tolerance:** nothing is flagged unless the caller supplies a tolerance %, because what is acceptable is the owner's call (brief: no hard-coded interpretations).
**Preserved:** shift open/close, stock posting, cash controls unchanged. Old PumpRequest/PumpResponse constructors kept; an older client that sends no grouping does not ungroup a pump (send an empty dispenser label to clear).
**Files:** V665; FuelPump, ForecourtDtos, FuelRetailDtos, FuelForecourtService, FuelTankReconciliationService, FuelForecourtController, FuelStockMovementRepository.findByTankBetween, FuelShiftReadingRepository.sumMeterLitresForTank; web TankReconciliationPanel, tankReconciliationOps, forecourtOps, ForecourtTab.
**DB (V665, additive):** fuel_pumps.dispenser_label, nozzle_number; checks and a partial unique index. Checked on scratch PostgreSQL 16.
**API:** GET /api/v1/fuel/forecourt/tanks/{id}/reconciliation?from&to&tolerancePct (FUEL_READ/FUEL_FORECOURT/FUEL_MANAGE, module guard). Pump create/update accept dispenserLabel, nozzleNumber.
**Tests:** FuelTankReconciliationServiceTest (7), +3 in FuelForecourtServiceTest; web: TankReconciliationPanel (6), forecourtOps +1, ForecourtTab +1. Vitest 240 passed in fuel folder. Java parse-checked only.
**Gaps:** reconciliation uses dips as the physical truth, so it is only as good as the dips recorded; movements are cut at dip timestamps (a shift closing a minute after the closing dip falls outside); no PDF export; no scheduled variance alert (still not built); no per-nozzle meter check against sales value.
